## How I build with AI

- **Private first, public through a check.** Every repo starts private. Before the switch, its whole history, every image and all metadata are scanned for anything personal.
- - **Nothing ships without my yes.** Deploys, settings and keys stay with me. The assistant proposes; I approve.
  - - **Each project keeps its own record:** the rules the AI works under, what was decided and why, and what independent reviews found.
    - - **Only what the thing needs.** Native apps where it matters, one HTML file where it doesn't.
     
      - Some builds stay private on purpose: anything that touches my mail, my calendar or my family's data. Write-ups of those are coming.
